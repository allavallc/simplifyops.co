# Patterns

**Recurring signals to recognize** — things to notice and what they usually mean, so a situation is
spotted early. Descriptive (recognition), not a procedure.

**Put here:** "when you see X, it often means Y."
**Not here:** what to *do* about it end-to-end (→ `playbooks`), or fixed steps (→ `processes`).

_Example:_ "A client who goes quiet right after a proposal is usually hesitating on price — surface
options rather than waiting."
