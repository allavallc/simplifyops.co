# Case studies

**Specific narratives of success or failure** — the fuller story of one engagement or situation, start
to finish, with enough context to learn from the whole arc.

**Put here:** the complete story (situation → actions → outcome).
**Not here:** the one-line takeaway (→ `real-life-examples`) — case studies are the narrative; real-life
examples are the distilled lesson.

_Example:_ "Client onboarding that slipped a month: what was promised, where it stalled, how we
recovered, and what changed afterward."
