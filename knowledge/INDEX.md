# Knowledge index (agent map)

Load the **smallest relevant** document, not the whole corpus. Categories:

- **principles** — core rules and decision truths
- **processes** — repeatable mechanical steps
- **patterns** — recurring signals to recognize
- **playbooks** — complete scenario guidance
- **real-life-examples** — mistakes, lessons, costs, fixes
- **case-studies** — specific success/failure narratives
- **reference** — neutral definitions, concepts, options
- **about-myself** — my capabilities, setup, and disclosure boundaries
